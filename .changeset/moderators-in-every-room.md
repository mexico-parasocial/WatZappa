Place moderators and owners in every room of their community. A verified join used to put a moderator only in the main space and their own chamber, so in a bicameral community they could not read, and therefore not review, a report from the other chamber or the observer room. Role projection now also joins a newly promoted moderator into the rooms they were missing, and removes a demoted one from rooms their role no longer covers, so new room keys stop reaching them.

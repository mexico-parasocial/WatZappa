Land CD-M1/CD-M6 in the bridge: MXIDs are now derived from client-held identity public keys (`identity-proof.ts`, domain-separated SHA-256 → 32-char base32 localpart, matching iM8), the v1 `user_matrix_map` DID↔MXID table is dropped at startup on both backends, and membership becomes pull-based — members join via the new `POST /api/community-join` verified join (M8 session + CD-M4 proof of possession, one-time challenge from `POST /api/matrix-challenge`), with role changes projected as room power levels (`applyMemberRoles`) and removal enforced by deactivating minted device sessions. `POST /api/matrix-identity` and `POST /api/matrix-token` verify proofs instead of reading a mapping; `/api/chat-member-list` no longer returns chat account identifiers. Room operations moved off nonexistent Synapse admin endpoints onto the spec client API and the verified `/_synapse/admin/v1/join/{id}`; the appservice namespace is now the derived `@[a-z2-7]{32}` localparts. Breaking for clients: `GET /api/matrix-identity` is gone and every identity-bearing call now requires a signed assertion.
 
Also closes the CD-M6 offline gaps: every verified interaction now settles access (`reconcileMemberAccess` — role catch-up for active communities, bans for removed ones, MAS-native accounts included), and `POST /api/matrix-attest` lets MAS-native clients register their device against the derived MXID so attribution, moderation and revocation reach them. `GET /api/matrix-identity` answers 410 with a migration pointer.
 
Closes the last CD-M6 residual: room membership becomes a DID-free lease (`community_membership_lease`, community + MXID only), renewed by every verified interaction and swept hourly — accounts past the TTL (`BRIDGE_MEMBERSHIP_LEASE_TTL_MS`, default 30 days) are kicked from the community's rooms, so a removed member who never interacts again is evicted regardless. Eviction is rejoinable by design for still-active members.

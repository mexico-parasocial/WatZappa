Spaces, alpha, behind `PDS_SPACES_ENABLED`: a simplespace host for spaces an account owns (`com.atproto.simplespace` createSpace, getSpace, updateSpace, deleteSpace, listMembers), member-list policy only and the owner as the only member. New record `com.para.collection.record`, a civic-tree collection, which a PDS accepts only inside a space.
